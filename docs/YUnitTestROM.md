## Extra content

The test ROM is itself an addition: it has its own MRA, and the OSD's Hidden Items page shows an "Octopus Bookkeeping" item for it that does nothing here (details below).

The [core-wide extras](ExtraContent.md#core-wide-features) (CRT Adjust, CRT V-Size, Video Timing, DIP Switches, Service Mode and the rest) apply to every game.

Midway's board diagnostic for the Y Unit, run with the Super High Impact
board settings and controls. Its tests are picked with the DIP switches in the
OSD. Its sound and graphics ROMs were never dumped, so it has no sound and no
graphics to check.

The OSD's Hidden Items page shows "Octopus Bookkeeping" for it too (it shares
Super High Impact's settings); it does nothing here.
